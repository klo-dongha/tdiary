T's Diary — Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This page covers consumer health data under laws such as the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada SB 370. Our full privacy policy is here.

  • What the app accesses. With your permission, the app reads sleep, step and workout data from Apple Health on your iPhone. It does not write to Apple Health.
  • Why. Only to show that day's sleep, steps and workouts in your diary on your iPhone.
  • Where it goes. Nowhere. The data stays on your iPhone. We never collect, receive, share or sell it, and it is never used for advertising. Sleep and step values are kept in a file excluded from iPhone backups; workouts are not stored. If you turn on "Include health values" when exporting, sleep and steps are added to the text you share yourself.
  • Third parties. None. No third party receives your health data from the app or from us.
  • Your choices. Turn off Health access in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › T's Diary, or turn off the health items in the app's Settings › What to record. Delete the app's records in Settings › My records › Delete all data.
  • Your rights. You may ask us to confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, to access it, to delete it, or to withdraw consent. Because we never receive it, our answer will confirm that we hold none. Write to wehahee@gmail.com. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General.
